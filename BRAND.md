# BRAND.md: ALLIET Software Labs

## 1. Core Identity & Positioning
- **Name:** ALLIET Software Labs
- **Domain:** alliet.company
- **Contact:** contact@alliet.company
- **Core Proposition:** "Ideas to Intelligent Products." We are a software lab building AI-native products, automation systems, and modern digital experiences for ambitious teams.
- **Positioning:** An engineering and product studio, not just a "freelancing company." We build both client solutions and our own software products.
- **Differentiator:** Real design + real engineering + real performance. We focus on technical sophistication, premium aesthetic, and high-performance execution.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary:** International startups, founders, product teams, and businesses needing custom software, AI implementation, or automation.
- **Secondary:** Indian startups, SMEs, and technology teams.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality:** Confident, intelligent, premium, restrained, editorial, and technical.
- **Tone:** Direct and concrete. No buzzwords, no fluff.
- **Do's:** Use concrete language ("We build AI systems, digital products and automation that solve real operational problems"). Be concise.
- **Don'ts:** Do not use cliché marketing speak ("revolutionizing the future", "cutting-edge solutions", "passionate team").

## 4. Visual Identity Principles
- **Aesthetic:** Editorial technology studio. Architectural, minimal, human-designed, and sophisticated.
- **Composition:** Asymmetric compositions, generous whitespace, precise alignment, and clean grid structures.
- **Color:** Restrained. Monochromatic foundation (off-whites, grays, deep darks) with carefully selected accent color (vibrant blue from the logo). No generic neon gradients or dark-mode-everything defaults.
- **Imagery:** Architectural imagery, technical diagrams, product screenshots, subtle textures. Avoid generic AI glowing brains or 3D robots.
- **Motion:** Intentional, subtle, performant. Scroll-triggered reveals, smooth transitions, spring-based micro-interactions. Avoid constant movement or scroll hijacking.

## 5. Key Content Architecture
1. **Work (Client Solutions):** "We build for others." Case study format, problem/approach/technology/result.
2. **Products (Internal Solutions):** "We build what we believe should exist." Dedicated section for ALLIET-owned products.
3. **Services:** Grouped into AI Systems, Digital Products, Automation, Software Engineering.
4. **Insights / About:** Focused on the product mindset, engineering, and shipping.

## 6. Handoff & Implementation Directives
- **Performance:** Excellent Core Web Vitals. Minimal JS, optimized assets.
- **SEO:** Built-in semantic HTML, schema markup, dynamic metadata.
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, minimal third-party libraries.
